# Glossary (illustrative, fictional)

## TVPI
Total Value to Paid-In capital. TVPI = (residual NAV + cumulative distributions) / paid-in capital. It combines realised and unrealised value.

## DPI
Distributions to Paid-In capital. DPI = cumulative distributions / paid-in capital. It measures cash actually returned to investors.

## NAV
Net Asset Value. The fair value of the fund's holdings at a reporting date, net of debt, attributable to the fund's ownership.

## Unrealised MOIC
Multiple on invested capital for a holding that has not been sold. Unrealised MOIC = NAV / cost.

## Paid-in capital
Cumulative capital called from investors and invested, measured in USD millions.

## Quarantine
A record that fails a critical data quality rule. It is excluded from curated tables and listed in the dq_quarantine table until corrected.
