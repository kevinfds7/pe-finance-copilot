# Valuation policy (illustrative, fictional)

## Frequency
Portfolio companies are valued quarterly. Marks are prepared within 60 days of quarter end.

## Methods
Approved methods are EV/EBITDA multiple, discounted cash flow, and recent transaction price. The method used must be recorded with every mark.

## Review thresholds
A mark above 15 times cost, or a quarter-on-quarter change above 30 percent, must be reviewed by the valuation committee before use in investor reporting.

## Currency
All figures are reported in USD millions. Financials in another currency must be converted before loading.
